Grok Bot: a team of AI helpers

Think of it as a small staff that works for you in the background. Tap any card to see an example.

What it can do

Example: You ask: “Find a time next week for coffee with my old roommate.” It checks your calendar, suggests three slots, and drafts the invite for you to approve.

Example: You ask which of two vacuum cleaners lasts longer. You get a short brief with links to reviews, so you can check it yourself.

Example: You say “Write a polite note asking the landlord to fix the heater.” It drafts it; nothing is sent until you tap approve.

Example: You want a long weekend somewhere warm. It lays out two or three options with travel times and a rough plan for each day.

Example: You need a new phone case. It compares a few, notes the trade-offs, and waits for you before anything is purchased.

Example: Every morning it could check whether a store has an item back in stock and only ping you when it does.

Example: You jot down how you slept this week. It spots patterns and helps you prepare questions for your doctor.

Example: After a planning chat, it turns the ideas into a task list with due dates so nothing slips.

Example: It builds you a spreadsheet comparing apartments, or a one-page summary like this one.

Example: It reads a receipt in your email and files it in the right Drive folder.

Example: You mention once that you prefer aisle seats. Next trip, it already knows.

Example: The researcher gathers facts, the writer turns them into a draft, and the reviewer checks it before you see it.

Example: Not sure who should handle something? Ask the chief of staff. Know exactly who you want? Message that bot directly.

One front door, many specialists

You can talk to any bot directly, or ask the chief of staff to route the job to the right one.

You stay in charge. By default, anything that goes out (an email, a message, a purchase, a trade) waits for your yes.
